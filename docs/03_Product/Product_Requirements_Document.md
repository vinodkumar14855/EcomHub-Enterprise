# 1. Product Overview

EcomHub Enterprise is a scalable multi-vertical digital platform that enables customers to discover, purchase, book, and manage different categories of products and services.

The platform supports:

- Product marketplace

- Professional services

- Travel booking

- Insurance marketplace

- Digital services

The platform connects:

- Customers

- Vendors

- Service Providers

- Travel Partners

- Insurance Partners

- Administrators

through one unified ecosystem.

---

# 2. Product Goals

## Primary Goals

- Create a trusted digital marketplace

- Enable local businesses to sell online

- Provide customers a single platform for multiple needs

- Digitize traditional businesses

- Increase vendor revenue opportunities

- Create scalable commerce infrastructure
