# 1. Platform Overview Workflow

EcomHub connects:

Customer → Platform → Vendor / Service Provider / Travel Partner / Insurance Partner

Overall flow:

```

User Discovery

        ↓

Registration/Login

        ↓

Browse Product or Service

        ↓

Selection

        ↓

Transaction / Booking

        ↓

Payment

        ↓

Fulfillment

        ↓

Review & Feedback

```

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

- Create customer account

- Generate customer ID

- Send welcome notification
